About Island Landscape

Island Landscape is a locally owned lawn and garden service established in 1995. As a proud Plant Amnesty member, Island Landscape uses proper, professional landscape management practices to keep your yard healthy, safe and looking its best.

We serve both residential and commercial clients throughout the North Seattle area, including Shoreline, Edmonds, Kenmore, Brier, Lake Forest Park and Lynnwood, as well as surrounding areas. Whether you need a one-time service or a regular maintenance plan, we customize our work to fit your property’s needs.
